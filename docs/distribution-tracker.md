## Verification notes

- AlternativeTo requires an account with verified email before submitting; normal review is free but has a large backlog and no promised review date. Priority review is paid and is excluded from this plan.
- AlternativeTo's terms say it may use submitted product name, icon, screenshots, and materials in its listing. Confirm assets before uploading.
- FileHorse requests product/version, official website, installer download link and license; its terms grant use of the product name/icon/screenshots and allow it to link to or mirror installation files.
- Software Informer says new/update submission requires login; it supports PAD submissions. Do not proceed to the login step without authorization.
- Search-engine checks did not confirm listings at Softpedia, FileHorse, or Software Informer. This is not proof that no listing exists.
